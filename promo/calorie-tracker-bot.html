Calorie Tracker Bot

AI Vision + Voice input + Referrals

Overview

Telegram bot that tracks daily calorie goal with text, photo (OCR/Vision) and voice input. Stores timezone and resets counter daily for the user. Viral growth through referrals.

Key features
  • Text, photo OCR and voice messages
  • Daily goals and timezone‑aware reset
  • Referral system and reminders
Delivery notes

The tracker is built for everyday logging rather than generic health content. We define how users submit meals, how uncertainty is shown, how history is stored, and where the bot should ask clarifying questions instead of guessing.

When needed, the bot can connect to a nutrition database, export reports, support reminders, and keep separate profiles for different goals. The wording stays conservative: it helps track data but does not replace medical advice.

Safeguards
  • Confidence labels for AI-estimated calories and ingredients
  • Privacy-aware storage for food logs and user profile data
  • Clear non-medical positioning for weight and nutrition guidance
