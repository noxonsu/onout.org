Overview

Corporate AI system for automated review of contracts. Detects risks, checks compliance with policies, and generates detailed reports for lawyers. Based on client data the system typically reduces review time from hours to minutes.

Key features
  • Batch analysis of PDF/DOC/DOCX/RTF/TXT
  • Risk scoring, policy checks, explainable findings
  • Admin panel with roles, audit logs, and analytics
  • API for DMS/CRM/ERP integrations
Case study

International law firm automated contract analysis and cut review time by 90% while improving quality by 40%.

Tech stack & deliverables

Python, NLP models (OpenAI/Claude/Gemini), FastAPI/Node, Docker/K8s, on‑prem or cloud. Deliverables: running system, admin UI, API, documentation, source code, deployment and onboarding.

Target audience & use cases

Legal teams at enterprises, banks and corporates. Use cases: risk and compliance checks, contract templating QA, mass reviews during M&A.
