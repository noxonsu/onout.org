Overview

Delivery notes

The assistant is built around the company's real interview style: tone, role requirements, evaluation criteria, and escalation rules. It can prepare candidate questions, summarize interviews, and help recruiters keep feedback consistent.

We avoid positioning the tool as an automated hiring judge. The recommended setup keeps final decisions with human recruiters and uses the AI layer for preparation, note structuring, and repeatable communication.

Safeguards
  • Human ownership of hiring decisions and candidate communication
  • Structured evaluation templates instead of opaque scoring only
  • Privacy notes for candidate data, transcripts, and retention
