AI Psychotherapist

Support via natural conversations

Overview

Portfolio bot for therapeutic conversations in Telegram. Provides 24/7 confidential support and can be adapted to your brand voice and protocols.

Delivery notes

This bot is positioned as a conversational support product, not as a replacement for licensed mental health care. During setup we define the tone, allowed topics, emergency boundaries, privacy expectations, and when the bot must recommend human help.

The implementation can support guided journaling, mood check-ins, psychoeducation content, and escalation messages. Sensitive claims are kept conservative, and the owner receives documentation for reviewing prompts and conversation flows.

Safety boundaries
  • Clear disclaimer that the bot does not provide diagnosis or emergency care
  • Escalation copy for self-harm, crisis, or high-risk messages
  • Privacy-aware retention settings for conversation history
