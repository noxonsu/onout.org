Overview

  • RU/EN recognition with technical terms
  • AI cleanup and auto‑paste to active window
  • History of transcriptions and API
Benefits

  • Multilingual messaging and AI chats
Delivery notes

The app can be tuned for meeting notes, call summaries, field reports, or content drafting. We define audio limits, languages, speaker labels, export formats, and how users should correct recognition mistakes.

Production builds can include background uploads, queue processing, summaries, searchable transcripts, and integrations with CRM, helpdesk, or document storage. This makes speech recognition part of a workflow rather than a one-off converter.

Quality controls
  • Visible transcript confidence and manual correction path
  • Storage and retention settings for uploaded audio
  • Retry handling for long files and provider timeouts
