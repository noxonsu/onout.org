Overview

B2B platform to build and sell interactive AI avatars (coaching, consulting, sales). Includes avatar constructor, billing/subscriptions, templates and API for websites and messengers.

Key features
  • Avatar builder with video integration
  • Billing and subscriptions (Stripe/PayPal)
  • Templates for popular niches
  • Admin dashboard and REST API
Delivery notes

We treat an avatar platform as a product workflow, not as a visual demo. The build starts with user roles, conversation goals, billing rules, moderation needs, and the places where the avatar must appear: website, mobile app, messenger, or CRM.

The production version can include branded prompts, template libraries, session history, analytics, admin controls, and escalation to a human operator when the conversation becomes sensitive or commercially important.

Safeguards
  • Clear boundaries for advice, sales claims, and customer support answers
  • Conversation logging with privacy-aware retention settings
  • Human review points for high-risk or high-value interactions
