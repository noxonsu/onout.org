Delivery notes

The AML checker is scoped around the team's compliance workflow: which chains to support, which providers to query, what risk levels mean, and how operators should handle uncertain results. The Telegram interface keeps checks fast without hiding the source response.

Production builds can include role-based access, team audit logs, saved checks, PDF or CSV export, and webhook delivery to a CRM or back-office system. We also document provider limits and fallback behavior.

Compliance safeguards
  • Visible provider, timestamp, risk score, and checked address in every result
  • Escalation rules for medium-risk or incomplete responses
  • Logs that support internal review without exposing unnecessary user data
