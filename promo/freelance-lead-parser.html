Freelance Lead Parser

Qualified opportunities straight to Telegram

Overview

AI parser that selects only relevant FL.ru projects, qualifies clients, and sends a ready brief to Telegram. Helps sales to focus on high‑quality leads.

Benefits

Reduces manual sift‑through, increases quality of outreach, and speeds up response time.

Delivery notes

The parser is tuned to the projects a team actually wants to win. We define target marketplaces, budget thresholds, required skills, exclusion keywords, language filters, and how leads should be scored before they reach Telegram or CRM.

The system can keep a history of seen projects, explain why a lead was selected, and draft a short response for manual review. This avoids noisy alerts and keeps the workflow useful for daily business development.

Controls
  • Duplicate detection across repeated marketplace posts
  • Scoring rules that can be changed without redeploying the parser
  • Manual approval before any client-facing outreach
